## Project info


Fitscan is a calorie tracking and wellness application designed to assist individuals in maintaining their health and gaining control over their eating habits.

PROBLEM - Monitoring your food intake is extremely crucial for leading a healthy lifestyle and managing our eating behaviors. However, many of us have experienced traditional applications that necessitate manual entry of details, making it challenging to adhere to this practice over time.

SOLUTION - FITSCAN is a calorie tracking application that utilizes artificial intelligence to automatically analyze photos uploaded to the website, eliminating the need for manual input. This significantly simplifies the process of tracking your calories and other essential macronutrients. It automatically adjusts to your requirements based on your body and objectives, whether your aim is fat loss or muscle gain, and configures itself accordingly for your specific goal.
